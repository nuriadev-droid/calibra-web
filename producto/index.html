    Questions

    What counts as a credit?

    One credit is one workflow run, however many steps it has. A run with 3 steps and a run with 60 steps both use one credit. Only very long runs use more: one extra credit for every further 100 steps.

    Do AI steps cost extra?

    You choose. Use the AI included in Calibra, which is paid with credits, or connect your own provider key and pay the model directly with no markup from us. Either way, Calibra suggests cheaper models for steps that don't need an expensive one.

    Can I try Calibra without stopping my current tool?

    Yes. Import your workflows, then run them in shadow mode: Calibra processes the same live events while your current tool keeps working, and steps that send, write or charge return sample data so nothing is duplicated. Switch when the results match.

    Can AI agents spend money or send things on their own?

    Actions that move money, such as payments, refunds, payouts and transfers, never run from an AI assistant or agent. For anything else you can add an approval step, so the workflow waits for a person's OK.

    Can I white-label Calibra for my clients?

    Yes. Put your name, logo and colours on the app, give each client their own workspace with a credit budget, and install your solutions for new clients from private templates.

    Where is my data stored?

    On servers in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the European Union. Run data is kept for 30 days by default, and admins can shorten that or delete it right after each run.

    Which currency do you charge in?

    Prices are in US dollars. Customers in Europe can choose to pay in euros when they subscribe.

    Do I need a card to start?

    No. The free plan is free forever and needs no card. You only add a payment method when you choose a paid plan, and you can cancel at any time.

    Which languages does the app support?

    The app opens in English and can be switched to Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Dutch, Japanese, Chinese and Arabic.
