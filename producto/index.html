    Questions

    What counts as a credit?

    One credit for each step that works with an app or runs code, with a minimum of one per run. Routers, loops, branches and the logic and data tools (text, numbers, dates, data mapping, CSV, waits and approvals) are free.

    Do AI steps cost extra?

    AI steps, the copilot and AI agents use your own key from your AI provider, so you pay the model cost directly to them with no markup from us. Calibra also suggests cheaper models for steps that don't need an expensive one.

    Can I bring my workflows from another automation tool?

    Yes. Import them from the migration page and you get a compatibility report that shows which steps moved over and which ones need a look.

    Where is my data stored?

    On servers in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the European Union. Run data is kept for 30 days.

    Do I need a card to start?

    No. The free plan is free forever and needs no card. You only add a payment method when you choose a paid plan, and you can cancel at any time.

    Which languages does the app support?

    The app opens in English and can be switched to Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Dutch, Japanese, Chinese and Arabic.
